Cosmic Discovery #01

30 Doradus Nebula

30 Doradus is a large nebula where new stars are born. It contains a huge group of young stars called R136 and is located in a nearby galaxy called the Large Magellanic Cloud.

Cosmic Discovery #02

Stars in the Galactic Core

These colorful stars are near the center of our Milky Way galaxy, about 26,000 light-years from Earth. This region contains old, red giant stars and younger, white stars like our Sun.

Cosmic Discovery #03

Spiral Galaxy NGC 2442

NGC 2442 is a galaxy with an unusual shape, so it is called the Meathook Galaxy. A star in this galaxy exploded in a supernova called SN 2015F.

Cosmic Discovery #04

Irregular Galaxy I Zwicky 18

This galaxy looks young, but Hubble found old stars inside it. This suggests that it may be as old as most other galaxies.
